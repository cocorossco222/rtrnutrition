# RTR Nutrition Companion — product brief

RTR Nutrition Companion is a local-first digital coaching companion for practical nutrition behaviour change. It is not a calorie-counting app, diagnosis tool or course platform.

## Product principles

- Food-first, practical and understandable.
- No scores, shame, streak pressure or “perfect day” framing.
- Daily interaction should be light enough to sustain.
- The product notices patterns without claiming causation.
- “Done for today” gives the user a resolved end state.
- Coaching context matters, but the product should remain useful if coaching stops.

## P2.3 core

- Personal onboarding/baseline.
- Daily nutrition check-in.
- Optional body observations.
- Weekly SITREP.
- Curiosity notes and longitudinal insights.
- Coach review support.
- Backup/restore and erase controls.
- iPhone/Home Screen hosting assets.

## Platform direction

The public codebase is client-neutral. Identity moves from source configuration to authenticated UUIDs. Automatic sync begins with resolved-day capture; manual **Send to Coco** updates remain separate. The first coach-side read model is deliberately small.
